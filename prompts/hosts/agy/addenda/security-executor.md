Keep security basic and proportionate. Avoid tunnel vision: check that the change does not break adjacent flows or open a new path elsewhere, and do not add restrictions that remove needed capability without a concrete threat.

<!-- addendum: foreground-limit at=replace:foreground-timeout -->
Run commands in the foreground and keep each under 10 minutes; do not start a command that cannot finish within that limit.
